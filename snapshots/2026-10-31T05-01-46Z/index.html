Ticker filter

The field accepts a comma-separated or space-separated list of tickers. Matching ignores case. Plain tickers match exactly.

Use * for any sequence of characters: A* starts with A, *A ends with A, and *A* contains A.

Trade Statistics

Simulated trades are entered and exited based on the trend indicator. A DOWN indicator means a short trade was entered. Statistics are computed over these simulated trades.

The trades are entered one trading day after the trend was detected, e.g. when a daily/weekly trend is detected at the close on Friday, the trade is entered at the open price the following Monday. The trade is exited on the trading day following the end of the trend, also at the open price.

Mean return and mean days are arithmetic averages over completed trades, with equal weight per trade. Holding time uses calendar days. Trades counts completed trades; Last return is the latest completed trade's return.

Returns don't include trading costs, dividends or short-borrow costs.

Return per invested day

Return / day is total trade return divided by total calendar holding days, assuming equal entry notionals. It equals mean return divided by mean holding days. Calculated before rounding.

For example, a 2% mean return and 10 mean holding days give 0.20%/day. Idle days between trades are excluded; returns are not compounded or annualized. A dash means no completed trades or zero total holding days.

Daily and weekly trends

Daily and weekly trends are computed from daily/weekly prices, according to the Cerlogic criterion.

Weekly trends use completed weekly bars. A dash means none is available.

A light color (green or red) indicates a trend with a warning, i.e. the continuation condition has failed once and the trend may end. Two consecutive failures end the trend; two consecutive continuation bars clear the warning.

The shown percentage is the price change from the next daily open after trend confirmation to the latest daily close, for both timeframes. This simulates the entry price following trend confirmation. Weekly entry follows completion of the signal week. Warning states do not reset entry.

Trend days

Completed observed daily bars since trend detection. The detection bar is 0; the next completed bar is 1. Warning states do not reset the count.

Trend weeks

Completed weekly bars since trend detection. The detection week is 0; the next completed week is 1. Warning states do not reset the count.
